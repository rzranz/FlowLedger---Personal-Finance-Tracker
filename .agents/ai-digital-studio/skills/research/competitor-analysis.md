# Skill: Competitor Analysis

## 1. Purpose
Compare relevant competitors on defined dimensions using sourced evidence.

## 2. Used By
- `roles/research-analyst.md`

## 3. Required Inputs
- Defined market or niche
- Competitor list or criteria to find them
- Comparison dimensions (offer, pricing, UX, messaging, SEO)

## 4. Procedure
1. Confirm scope and dimensions.
2. Identify 3 to 8 relevant competitors and justify selection.
3. Collect evidence per dimension and log each source with access date.
4. Build a comparison matrix.
5. Extract patterns, gaps, and opportunities.
6. State confidence and what could not be verified.

## 5. Outputs
- Competitor matrix
- Source log
- Insight summary with opportunities and risks

## 6. Quality Criteria
- Each cell has a source or is marked unknown.
- Selection criteria are stated.
- Insights follow from the evidence.

## 7. Boundaries
- Do not fabricate evidence, sources, results, or file paths.
- Stay within the task's scope and permissions.
- Report limitations and unverified items.
