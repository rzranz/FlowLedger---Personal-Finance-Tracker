# Skill: Content Planning

## 1. Purpose
Plan messaging and a content structure tied to goals.

## 2. Used By
- `roles/content-strategist.md`
- `roles/marketing-growth.md`

## 3. Required Inputs
- Goal and audience
- Tone guidance
- Channels

## 4. Procedure
1. Define key messages and proof points.
2. Map audience needs to content angles.
3. Plan structure per page or channel.
4. Define format, length, and cadence.
5. Mark facts needing verification.
6. Produce plan and draft where requested.

## 5. Outputs
- Messaging framework
- Content plan
- Copy deck (when requested)

## 6. Quality Criteria
- Each item ties to a goal and audience need.
- Unsourced claims are flagged.
- Cadence is realistic for stated capacity.

## 7. Boundaries
- Do not fabricate evidence, sources, results, or file paths.
- Stay within the task's scope and permissions.
- Report limitations and unverified items.
